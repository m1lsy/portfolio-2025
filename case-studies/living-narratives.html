Living Narratives

Project

Erasmus+ Blended Intensive Programme

Role

Story development, filming and editing

Tools

CapCut, ArtLux and LiDAR interaction

The Brief

Living Narratives brought students from the Netherlands, France, Poland and Croatia together at L'École de Design Nantes Atlantique from 26 to 30 October 2026. We created an interactive story about Nantes using original photos, videos and sounds.

Visitors moved through an immersive room equipped with LiDAR sensors. Their position influenced the next video, creating a branching narrative rather than a fixed film.

Concept

Our team explored the contrast between wealth and poverty in Nantes. We wanted visitors to reflect on how different living conditions can exist within the same city.

How can interactive storytelling and immersive technology make a social issue feel immediate and personal?

My Contribution

I helped develop the story concept, filmed locations and edited the footage into short video states in CapCut. I also contributed to the visual direction, including the sticky-note-style text treatment, and helped my teammate Bruno work with ArtLux.

We chose to rely mainly on our own footage instead of generative AI because it kept the story grounded in the places and people we had actually encountered.

  • Story concept and narrative structure
  • Original footage collected in Nantes
  • Edited video sequences
  • ArtLux triggers connected to visitor movement

Final Experience

Watch the Living Narratives project video to see the visual style and branching immersive experience.

Outcome

The final immersive experience gave visitors eight possible endings. It combined video editing, spatial interaction and social storytelling in a format that asked people to influence the story physically rather than only watch it.

The project strengthened my skills in international collaboration, interactive storytelling, filming and editing. It also taught me to adapt quickly when requirements changed and to balance taking initiative with leaving space for teammates' ideas.
